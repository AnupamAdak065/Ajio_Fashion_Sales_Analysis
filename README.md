## 2. Introduction

Ajio Fashion Insight BI is a comprehensive analytics project designed to monitor and optimize retail performance. By combining high-level dashboard visualizations with granular SQL deep-dives, this project prioritizes immediate, actionable business outcomes—tracking pricing trends, discount depths, and market segmentation to maximize revenue.

## 3. Dataset Information

* **Total Products Analyzed:** 367,172
* **Total Brands Covered:** 1,975
* **Data Scope:** Complete Men's and Women's Fashion Collections
* **Data Availability:** Real-Time Analytics

## 4. Tools Used

* **Business Intelligence & Visualization:** Dashboarding tools (e.g., Power BI / Tableau)
* **Data Processing:** SQL for precise aggregations, tiering, and market segmentation.
* **Languages:** SQL, Python

## 5. Key Calculations & KPIs

* **Price Segmentation:** Products categorized into Budget (<1k), Mid-Range (1k-5k), Premium (5k-20k), Luxury (20k-50k), and Super Luxury (50k+).
* **Average Discount %:** Evaluates promotional intensity per brand.
* **Category Pricing:** Tracks average discounted prices across core apparel types (Shirts, Bottomwear, T-Shirts).
* **Highest Average Profit %:** Tracks top margin contributors.

## 6. Detailed Observation & Drive Growth

**Key Metric Observations:**

* **Market Dominance by Tier:** The **Mid-Range (1k - 5k)** tier dominates the men's catalog, accounting for **74.10%** of total products (115,323 items). Budget items make up 18.57%, while Super Luxury is highly exclusive at just **0.02%**.
* **High-Ticket Outliers:** Men's fashion features a massive premium peak with *zegna* dominating the Super Luxury tier (reaching original prices up to 230,800). Women's premium is led by *suket-dhir*.
* **Category Value:** Men's Bottomwear commands a significantly higher average discounted price (**1,924**) compared to T-Shirts (**925**).
* **Discounting Extremes:** *aestheticmen-clothing* leads men's discounts at an aggressive **80.63%**, followed closely by *9-impression* at **79.00%**.

**Actionable Steps to Drive Growth:**

1. **Optimize Core Inventory:** Since 74.1% of the catalog sits in the 1k-5k range, marketing spend should be heavily optimized for this "sweet spot" demographic to drive volume.
2. **Strategic Cross-Selling:** Pair higher-AOV items like Bottomwear (~1,924) with lower-AOV items like T-Shirts (~925) in bundled promotions to increase overall cart value.
3. **Margin Protection & Audits:** Brands discounting at 75-80%+ (e.g., *aestheticmen-clothing*, *sports-52-wear*) should be audited. Adjusting these aggressive markdown strategies can help push the baseline profit margin closer to the 17% top-tier average.
4. **Premium Upselling:** Utilize Super Luxury brands like *Zegna* and *Suket-Dhir* as halo marketing tools to elevate brand perception, targeting VIP customers.
